Deltarune All Chapters Retimer (for YouTube)

About

A tool for retiming "All Chapters" runs of Deltarune. It can also be used to retime any game or category that is made up of multiple segments. It gets the time for each chapter, the real time (RTA) for the run, and the in-game time (IGT, sum of each chapter time).
